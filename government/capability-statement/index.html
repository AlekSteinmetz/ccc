Company overview

Coral Construction Company is a family-owned contractor founded in 1968 and headquartered in Marathon, at the center of the Florida Keys. We deliver marine, structural, building and public works construction throughout Monroe County, with family leadership on every job site.

Core competencies

  • Roadway & Asphalt Repair
  • Sidewalks, Curb & ADA
  • Stormwater & Drainage
  • Utility Repair & Restoration
  • Bridges, Culverts & Structural Concrete
  • Seawalls & Public Waterfronts
  • Emergency & Storm Response
  • Public Facilities

Differentiators

  • Based near the midpoint of the Keys — same-day reach to any site
  • Over five decades of in-water and coastal construction
  • Family-supervised crews and direct access to ownership
  • Experience working in and around a national marine sanctuary
