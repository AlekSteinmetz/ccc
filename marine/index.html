Where the Keys meet the water

Marine work is the heart of what we do.

Every structure we build on the water is engineered around the forces it will face: tide and current, wave action, corrosion, marine borers and the occasional hurricane. That’s how a dock lasts for decades and a seawall holds when the surge comes.

02 — DOCKS

Docks built for decades, not seasons.

A dock in the Keys takes a beating. Choosing the right decking and piling is the difference between a structure that lasts and one that needs replacing in a decade. We build in every major material and help you choose the right one for your water, your boat and your budget.

04 — SPALL REPAIR

Stopping the Keys’ most common structural problem.

Saltwater works its way into concrete. The reinforcing steel inside rusts and expands, and the concrete cracks and breaks away — that’s spalling, and left alone it only gets worse.

We chip back to sound concrete, clean and treat the exposed steel, replace what’s too far gone, and rebuild the section to restore its strength — on docks, pile caps, seawalls, columns, beams, slabs and stairs.
